This article is written by Marijan Kozic Codemagic Patch ships live updates of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to Capacitor, Ionic, and Cordova-on-Capacitor apps, without a new store binary. Say a checkout button stops working on some Android 15 devices, and the fix turns out to be four lines of TypeScript. In a Capacitor app, those four lines live in the web layer, inside a WebView, nowhere near native code.
